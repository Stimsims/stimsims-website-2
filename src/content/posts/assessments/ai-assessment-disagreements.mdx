The on-device AI was unable to indicate what part of my answer earned the points, while the large AI both agreed which section earned the points. It's helpful when using AI for assessments to ask it to quote your work directly so you see if it's assessment is plausible, or hallucinated.

### Excitation and Inhibition of Neurons
Question: What determines a neurons action potential threshold, and why do different synapses have an excitatory or inhibitory effect on it?
Rubric: **Mechanism of Excitation/Inhibition**: Identify that the synaptic effect is determined by the **ionic permeability** and the resulting **reversal potential (**Erev​**)** of the activated ion channels compared to the action potential **threshold**,.
Answer: A neurons action potential is determined by its recent firing activity and it's unstable equilibrium between Na+ influx and K+ efflux. A postsynaptic transmitter can be ionotripic or metabotropic. When stimulated, they open channels that allow ions like Na+, K+ and Cl- into the neuron. If a post-synaptic receptor allows in ions like Na+ and K+ that are more positive than the neuron's action potential threshold, then they're excitatory as they move the neurons electrical potential upward towards this threshold. If the receptor opens channels that allow Cl- or other ions more negative than the electrical potential of the neuron, they have an inhibitory effect, bringing the electrical potential of the neuron further down, away from the action potential threshold.
