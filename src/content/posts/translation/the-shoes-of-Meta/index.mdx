### Why small models?
Small LLM’s lose their vocabulary and depth of meaning as they’re trained on less data. This means they encounter more words they don’t know the meaning or, or vaguely understand, and have to guess at how to translate them. This can reveal a mess of mathematical biases that can be very funny when aimed at articles about companies or industries. Avoid biases about groups of people, it pigeon holes humanity.
