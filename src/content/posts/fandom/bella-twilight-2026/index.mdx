## The Fans of Bella in Twilight
With a lexicon drenched in emotional words and communication, the twilight fans pay attention to problems of the heart that are surprising, terrible, horrible, guilty, insane and furious. Characters have strengths in resolving problems by understanding, answering, talking and explaining. There is a pre-occupation with beauty that values the uniqueness of people. The innocent and precious children in the stories appear to be plot devices, as they are newborn. 

#### Who is Bella?
Bella is the main protagonist of the Twilight Saga. She starts as a 17 year old girl in Washington, America. She falls in love with, and marries, the vampire Edward Cullent and becomes part of his coven. Their child is a human/vampire hybrid. Bella has special powers including immunity to vampire magic. [Twilight Saga Fandom](https://twilightsaga.fandom.com/wiki/Bella_Swan)

- The fandom: Twilight

- Sorted by: stories with the most likes
