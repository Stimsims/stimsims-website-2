## The Fans of Jason Todd in Batman
Fan's of Jason Todd stories are often about governance of business, using nouns like "government, debt, trade, insurance, devices, letters, email". The more unique verbs these characters use involve swimming, suffering ("slapped, stabbing, tortured") and making oaths of marriage ("promises, propose, marry"). Communication is framed as background chatter - "chattering, screeching, groaning, mumbling" - not of central importance. The world is often described using adjectives of timidity and aggression with "wary, fragile and unnerved" vs "brute, volatile and murderous". 

## Who is Jason Todd?
Jason Todd is the second sidekick Robin for Batman, first appearing in 1983. Jason was not popular, and was killed by the Joker based on a fan vote. Later, a plot line resurrected him and now he is depicted as an anti-hero to Batman. He is more willing to use lethal force.
